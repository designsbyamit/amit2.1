Where will the organisation put its effort?

Raise RFQs and map vendor responses with agents, quality checks before every award, and the pilot reaching two more plants.

Two or three things someone outside design would notice

Buyers stop re-keying quotes. Quality sign-off happens inside the flow. Product asks design for the next workflow before it is planned.

Roles we have

By level and capability

A senior interaction designer, a designer, a researcher and a design systems lead.

Roles we need

What is missing for sections 1 to 3

Where we depend on partners

Product, engineering, data, research, design systems

Specific enough to observe

Be specific. A recent hire is a good example.

Headcount, channels, tooling

Role Definition Worksheet

Turn a vague need into a clear role and a job description people want to answer.

Worked example · fictional company

NorthStar GmbH · A children’s food maker with an agent-assisted procurement pilot. The design team is hiring a Senior Conversation Designer.

How to use it

Work through sections 1 to 4 first. Only then write the job description in section 5.

1. Why this role exists

The problem this person will solve

What is not happening today that should be?

Agent-assisted workflows are being designed without anyone who specialises in how an agent speaks, asks and recovers.

What success looks like after twelve months

Three outcomes, written as things that will be true

1. Buyers at two plants use the agent-assisted RFQ flow. 2. Buyers can see and override what the agent did without help. 3. Product and engineering have adopted tone and recovery guidelines.

2. Level and scope

DimensionWhat we expect in this role
LevelSenior
Scope of ownershipConversation and agent behaviour across the procurement pilot
Decisions they make aloneWording, tone and recovery patterns
Decisions they influenceWhen the agent interrupts, and what it may do on its own
People or partners they work withBuyers, quality leads, product, engineering, data science
Reports toDesign lead, procurement experience

3. Must have, can learn, not needed

Must have on day one

Keep this short

Experience designing conversational or agent interfaces. Comfort testing with real users. Clear writing.

Can learn here

What we will teach

Our procurement domain, the supplier landscape and the internal design system.

Not needed

What we tend to ask for out of habit

A degree in a particular discipline. Years in the food industry. Five-plus years in one tool.

4. What we can honestly offer

The work, the people, the growth, and the trade-offs

The work: the first agent-assisted flows in procurement, with real buyers. The people: a small team, a product partner and a data science partner. The growth: a path to lead conversation design. The trade-off: ambiguity, because agent behaviour is still being defined.

5. The job description

Write it in this order. Plain language, no internal jargon, and no list longer than five lines.

Title and team

Use a title people search for

Senior Conversation Designer, Procurement Experience, NorthStar Design

Why this role exists

Two or three sentences from section 1

Buyers spend days chasing vendors. We are building agents that help, and need someone to design how they speak, ask and recover.

What you will do

Up to five outcomes, not tasks

Define how the agent talks to buyers and quality leads. Test conversations with real buyers. Design recovery for when the agent is wrong. Write guidelines others adopt. Partner with product and engineering.

What you bring

Only the must-haves from section 3

Experience in conversational or agent design. Evidence of testing with users. Clear writing.

What you will learn and who you will work with

Section 4, written to the candidate

You will learn procurement and how agents are built. You will work with a design lead, a product partner and data science.

How we hire

The stages and the time each takes, so nobody has to guess

A call, a portfolio conversation, a paid three-hour exercise, two team conversations, and a decision within two days. About three weeks in all.
