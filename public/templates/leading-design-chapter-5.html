ConcernWhat it means for our workThe rule we set
Data that may never enter a toolSupplier prices, buyer details and unreleased product plansNo customer or supplier data in any tool that is not approved and hosted in our region
Approved tools onlyOnly the three tools on the company listAny other tool needs a request to security. Personal accounts are not used for work
Explanation and audit trailDecisions the agent takes or suggests must be explainableEach AI-assisted decision shows its reason and is recorded

ZoneWhat belongs hereRules that apply

Red: do not usePersonal data in public tools, generated users in place of real research, shipping unreviewed outputDo not do this. Raise it with the lead.
