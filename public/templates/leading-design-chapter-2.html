Feedback Preparation Worksheet

Prepare feedback that is appreciative, objective and clear before you say a word.

How to use it

Fill this in before a feedback conversation. If you cannot complete the evidence and standard boxes in section 3, you are not ready to give the feedback.

1. The situation

Who, and when

The person, the date, the work or moment in question

What I want this conversation to achieve

One sentence

2. Acknowledge and appreciate

Always start here, whatever your relationship. Be specific about the effort, not only the result.

The effort I saw

What did they do, try or carry?

What I appreciate, and why it mattered

Name the effect on the team, the product or the user

3. The feedback, objectively

Replace “I think” and “I feel” with what you saw and what it is measured against. If you cannot name the standard, wait.

What I observed (evidence)

Facts: what was said, shown or shipped, with dates

The impact

On users, the work, the team or the goal

The standard or expectation

The level expectation, the goal, the agreed metric or a team tenet

What good looks like

The specific behaviour or output you would like to see next

4. Choose the method

5. Support and follow-up

What I will do to help

Time, access, an example, a pairing

When we will look again

A date, and what we will look at

6. Before I go in

After the conversation, write down what was agreed and send it to the person within a day.

MomentWhat it is forWho gives to whomHow oftenFormat

2. Our feedback tenets

Pick the rules that fit your team. Four to six is enough.

Our own tenets (write your own)

1. We acknowledge the effort first, always. 2. We give evidence and a standard, never opinion. 3. We give feedback within two days. 4. Feedback goes in every direction, including to me.

3. Roll it out

StepWhoBy when
Agree the tenets with the teamThe whole team, in the team meetingWeek 1
Share the one-page tenets and put them where we workMeWeek 1
Start weekly feedback in one-to-onesMeWeek 2
Ask the team for feedback on meMe, with an anonymous formWeek 4

Signals we will watch

For example: fewer surprises at reviews, people asking for feedback, feedback arriving sooner

Fewer surprises in reviews. People ask for feedback before I give it. Feedback arrives within two days. The team gives me feedback and I respond in the next team meeting.

Written, spoken, straight away, time to think
