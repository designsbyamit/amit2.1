AR Item Display Labels

A Display Label is the plain-language term shown to collections specialists everywhere in the application — the worklist, account summaries, insight cards, and all outbound communications.

The eight product defaults apply at onboarding. As a Customer Administrator, you may rename any Display Label at any time without approval.

Changes apply only to your deployment. Renaming a Display Label does not affect the underlying AR Item Type identifier, classification logic, AI model, or any other customer’s deployment. Labels are shown in this customer’s Collections experience. Save records the previous and new values in the audit log.
