Agent–Agent Interaction Toolkit

Everything you need to design, check and improve how AI agents work with other agents, whether the other agent belongs to another company or sits in the same system. Tested against a power-supply case study and seven other complex scenarios.

What do you want to do?

Part 1 · Guidelines

Twenty-two practices, four moments

The rules for designing a whole agent-to-agent experience, grouped by when they matter: at the start, during the work, when something goes wrong, and over time. They apply to the solution as a whole. The patterns are the building blocks you use to follow them. Each guideline shows how Sunnyvale Power Farms (SPF) and Riztech Corp (RTC) apply it, and Cases maps them onto agents.

Part 2 · Patterns and worksheets

Reusable flows, and the solutions they build

A pattern is a flow that comes up again and again, such as finding offers, first contact or sign-off. It is drawn as a journey across your agent, the other agent and a person. Several patterns combine into a solution, and the guidelines are the rules for designing that whole solution. Each pattern comes with a worksheet.

Part 3 · Playbook

Context, assess, analyse, resolve

Four steps that build on each other. Share the context and map your agents. Assess what you have, or plan what you will build. See the gaps and why they exist. Then resolve them with examples, worksheets and a brief. Your answers stay in this browser.

Cases

Tested on real-world-style situations

The guidelines were tried on a worked case study, then stress-tested on seven other complex multi-agent scenarios. Start with the case study, then see what each test found.

How to use it

Read the guidelines to learn the rules for a whole experience. Use the patterns as building blocks, and the solutions to see how several patterns combine. Use the playbook to check a system you have or are planning, and to decide what to fix first.
